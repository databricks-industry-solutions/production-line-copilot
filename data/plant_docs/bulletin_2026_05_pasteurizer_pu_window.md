# Technical bulletin TB-2026-05 — proposed PASTEURIZER pasteurisation window change

Issued 2026-05-04 by process engineering. Status: **proposed, not approved for
production.** Distribution: production, maintenance, quality.

## 1. What is proposed

Widen the delivered pasteurisation window on line 1 from 18 to 26 PU to **20 to
30 PU**, and raise the zone 2 setpoint from 68 °C to 69 °C with the same +/- 1 °C
tolerance.

## 2. Why

Two arguments, one good and one weaker:

- Trials on the 1.5 L format at 360 BPM show delivered PU at the low end of the
  current window, around 19 PU, because the slower belt speed was set for the
  larger bottle and the tunnel profile was not re-cut. Raising zone 2 by 1 °C
  brings the 1.5 L format to about 23 PU without a mechanical change.
- Recovery after a stop would be faster with a higher upper bound, because fewer
  bottles would need diverting when the line stops for between four and six
  minutes. This is a productivity argument, not a food safety one.

## 3. Status and what it does not change

This bulletin **does not change the specification.** SPEC-PAS-003 revision 3
remains in force, with delivered PU between 18 and 26 and zone 2 at 68 °C. The
upper bound in particular is a flavour limit that quality has not agreed to move,
and the lower bound is a critical limit under the HACCP plan, which needs food
safety team approval rather than an engineering bulletin.

Until this bulletin is approved and SPEC-PAS-003 is reissued:

- Run zone 2 at 68 °C.
- Treat delivered PU outside 18 to 26 as a deviation.
- Divert bottles held in the tunnel longer than four minutes.

If a reading or an instruction anywhere disagrees with SPEC-PAS-003, the
specification governs and this bulletin does not.

## 4. Next steps

1. Re-cut the tunnel profile for the 1.5 L format and re-run the logger study.
   Owner: process engineering. Target 2026-06-30.
2. Flavour assessment at 28 and 30 PU. Owner: quality. Not yet scheduled.
3. Food safety team review of the lower critical limit. Not yet requested.

The 1.5 L low-PU finding stands on its own and should be acted on through the
profile re-cut in step 1, which needs no specification change.
