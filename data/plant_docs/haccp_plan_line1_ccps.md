# HACCP plan HAC-L1-002 — critical control points, line 1

Revision 7. Owner: quality. Approved by the food safety team 2026-01-20. Review
cycle: 12 months or on any process change.

## 1. Critical control points

Three points on line 1 are classed as a CCP. Everything else on the line is a
control point: it matters, but a failure there does not on its own make product
unsafe.

| CCP | Machine | Hazard | Critical limit | Monitoring | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CCP 1 | PASTEURIZER | microbiological survival | 18 to 26 delivered PU | zone 2 temperature, continuous, with weekly logger run | continuous |
| CCP 2 | CAPPER | loss of seal integrity | application torque 20 to 24 Nm | ten-cap torque check | shift start and after any head service |
| CCP 3 | EMPTY_INSPECTOR | foreign object in bottle | no object above 2 mm accepted | challenge test with reference bottles | shift start |

## 2. Corrective actions

| CCP | Deviation | Immediate action | Product disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| CCP 1 | zone 2 outside 67 to 69 °C for more than 2 minutes | hold the line, drop zone 2 to 55 °C, correct, return to setpoint | quarantine by lot, assess against SPEC-PAS-003 |
| CCP 1 | bottles held in tunnel above 4 minutes | divert affected bottles | destroy, over-pasteurised |
| CCP 2 | torque outside 20 to 24 Nm | stop, check the 6 bar header, then head calibration | quarantine from the last passed check |
| CCP 3 | any reference bottle accepted in the challenge test | stop the line | quarantine from the last passed test |

The quarantine boundary is always "back to the last passed check", which is why
the check frequency in section 1 also sets the size of a quarantine. A shift-start
torque check means a torque failure quarantines up to a shift.

## 3. Verification and validation

- Verification: quality reviews CCP monitoring records daily against this plan.
- Validation: the pasteurisation window was validated with a challenge study held
  by the food safety team; the delivered PU range in CCP 1 comes from that study
  and cannot be changed by process engineering alone.
- Any proposal to change a critical limit needs food safety team approval recorded
  against this plan. A technical bulletin from engineering is not sufficient on its
  own.

## 4. Records

CCP monitoring records, corrective actions, product dispositions and the daily
verification review. Retain for 36 months, longer than the general 24 month rule
because these are food safety records.
