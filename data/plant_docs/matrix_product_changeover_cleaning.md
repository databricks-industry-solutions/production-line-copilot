# Cleaning matrix MTX-CLN-004 — product changeover cleaning requirement

Revision 3. Owner: hygiene with quality. Review cycle: 12 months or on any new
product introduction.

## 1. Product groups

| Group | Products | Allergen | Colour carry risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | still water, sparkling water | none | none |
| B | still lemon, still orange | none | low |
| C | cola, cola zero | none | high, caramel colour |
| D | dairy-protein shake, vanilla and chocolate | milk | high |

Group D is the only allergen-bearing group on line 1. Everything about this matrix
follows from that: the risk being managed is milk protein reaching a product that
does not declare it, and colour carry is a quality issue by comparison.

## 2. Requirement by transition

Read as "from the row, to the column".

| From \ To | A | B | C | D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | rinse | rinse | rinse | full clean in place |
| B | full clean in place | rinse | rinse | full clean in place |
| C | full clean in place | full clean in place | rinse | full clean in place |
| D | full clean in place with protein verification | full clean in place with protein verification | full clean in place with protein verification | rinse |

- **Rinse**: phases 1, 3 and 5 of SOP-CIP-002, about 24 minutes.
- **Full clean in place**: the complete six-phase sequence, about 64 minutes plus
  heat-up.
- **Protein verification**: as full clean in place, plus a protein-specific swab at
  the FILLER bowl outlet and at the PASTEURIZER zone 2 spray header, in addition to
  the usual hygiene swabs. Both must return not detected.

Every transition out of group D requires protein verification, including to
another allergen-free group and including where the next product is water. There
is no shortcut out of D.

## 3. Planning consequences

The matrix is why the production sequence within a day runs A, B, C, D and not in
any other order: it needs three rinses and one full clean rather than up to four
full cleans. A day that runs D in the middle costs about 80 extra minutes of
cleaning, which is more than a format changeover.

The LABELLER changes labels for every product change regardless of cleaning, 20
minutes, and that runs inside whichever cleaning window applies.

## 4. Verification and failure

A protein swab that detects is a line stop. Product made since the swab was taken
is quarantined by lot, and the full clean in place is repeated from phase 2 before
any further production. A detected result is reported to quality the same shift;
it is an allergen event, not a hygiene note.

## 5. Records

The transition, the cleaning level applied, swab results and the operator, in the
QMS against the batch that follows. Retain for 36 months, as an allergen control
record.
