# Calibration certificate CAL-2026-0417-FLW — FILLER flowmeters

Instrument set: 24 filling valve flowmeters, FILLER, line 1. Calibrated
2026-04-17. Next due 2026-10-17. Performed by the instrument workshop against a
reference vessel with a traceable certificate.

## 1. Method

Ten fills per valve into the reference vessel at the 1.0 L format setpoint of
1000 ml, at production temperature and at 6 bar header pressure. As-found values
recorded before any adjustment; as-left values recorded after. Expanded
uncertainty of the reference is 0.8 ml.

## 2. Results by valve group

| Valve group | As-found mean | As-found spread | Adjusted | As-left mean | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 6 | 999.6 ml | 1.8 ml | no | 999.6 ml | pass |
| 7 to 12 | 1000.2 ml | 2.1 ml | no | 1000.2 ml | pass |
| 13 to 18 | 996.4 ml | 4.6 ml | yes | 999.8 ml | pass after adjustment |
| 19 to 24 | 1000.5 ml | 2.4 ml | no | 1000.5 ml | pass |

All as-left values are inside the 1000 ml +/- 6 ml working tolerance in
SPEC-FILL-021, and no individual fill was below the 985 ml reject figure.

## 3. Findings worth acting on

Valves 13 to 18 were low as-found by 3.6 ml with more than double the spread of
the other groups. Two valve seals in that group showed wear at the seat. Both
were replaced before the as-left run.

This is the pattern described in the planned maintenance schedule: a weeping seal
reads as a low fill that bowl level control compensates for, so the average at the
machine display stays credible while product is lost. The group average was inside
tolerance as-found — the spread was the signal, not the mean.

## 4. Recommendation

Bring the FILLER valve seal replacement forward from its six-monthly interval to
four months for valve groups 13 to 18 only, and re-assess at the next
calibration. Recorded against SCH-MNT-001 for the next maintenance review.

## 5. Traceability

Reference vessel certificate held by the instrument workshop. Calibration record,
as-found and as-left data retained for 24 months and available to quality on
request.
